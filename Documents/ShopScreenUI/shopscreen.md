This is a great starting point. You have the functional skeleton of a roguelike upgrade screen (three choices, clear title, descriptions).

To transform this into a "Sarcastic Horror" experience that matches your Art Guide, we need to move away from clean rectangular UI elements and treat the interface itself as part of the hostile, cynical world.

Based on your **Art & Style Guide** (MonsterChromatic), here are specific suggestions for redesigning the shop screen:

### ---

**1\. The Concept: The "Shop" is a Trap/Crypt**

Instead of a generic overlay on the gameplay screen, the shop should feel like a specific, unpleasant location the player has stumbled into.

* **The Container:** Frame the three upgrade cards within something thematic.  
  * *Idea:* The three choices are presented on a dilapidated, rotting wooden stall set up in the graveyard.  
  * *Alternative Idea:* The choices are carved into the side of a massive, cracked crypt wall.  
* **The Background Overlay:** Instead of just darkening the gameplay screen (Image 2), add a heavy vignette with creeping shadows, fog, or thorny vines at the edges of the screen to make the moment feel claustrophobic.

### **2\. UI Element Design (The Cards)**

The current clean, translucent rectangles (Image 1\) clash with the "hand-painted," "thick black contours" style described in the guide.

* **Texture and Shape:** The cards should not be perfect rectangles. They should look like ripped parchment, cracked stone tablets, or rusted metal plates bolted together.  
* **Borders:** Give the cards thick, wobbly black outlines, similar to the characters and environment props.  
* **Color Palette:** Use the "dead, earthy shades (beige, brown, grey)" defined in the guide for the card backgrounds. They should look dirty.  
  * *Example:* The "Denial of Death" card could look like a blood-stained bandage or a piece of dried skin.

### **3\. Emphasizing the Sarcasm (Typography & Text)**

You already have a brilliant title: **"Prolonging the inevitable."** Let's lean into that.

* **Title Presentation:** Don't just have the text floating. Put it on a tattered banner hanging above the choices, or have it crudely etched into stone at the top of the screen.  
* **The "Shopkeeper" (Narrative UI):** The Art Guide mentions "Sarcastic Speech Bubbles" for enemies and "Cynical UI" messages.  
  * *Suggestion:* Add a non-player character (NPC) or entity at the top of the shop screen (e.g., a hooded shadow entity, a talking skull on a stick).  
  * *Function:* When the shop opens, this entity gets a shaky speech bubble with a cynical greeting like: *"Oh look, it's still alive. Want to buy another 30 seconds of existence?"* or *"Pick your poison. They all lead to the same place."*  
* **Flavor Text:** The descriptions currently are very functional ("Restore Health"). Try to inject sarcasm here too, as you did with the sub-text "(Ignore the inevitable)".  
  * *Giant Mallet change:* "Acquire or upgrade the Circle Hammer. (Compensating for something?)"

### **4\. Icons and Visual Feedback**

The placeholders for icons on the cards need to be filled in the game's distinct style.

* **The Style:** Icons must be hand-painted with heavy black outlines.  
* **The Vibe:** They should look slightly pathetic or cursed.  
  * *Health Icon:* Instead of a shiny red heart, make it a stitched-together, graying anatomical heart that looks barely functional.  
  * *Weapon Icons:* The weapons should look rusty, chipped, and heavy, matching the environment art.  
* **Hover/Select Animation:** The Art Guide mentions "Wobble Movement" and procedural animation for characters and props. Apply this to the UI. When the player hovers over a card, it shouldn't just glow; it should nervously wobble, shake, or perhaps drip slime.
