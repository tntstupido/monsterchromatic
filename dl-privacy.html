At Die Laughing, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection and use of personal data when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Die Laughing is a single-player survival game. We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) directly within the game unless you voluntarily provide it to us (e.g., by contacting us for support).

However, we may collect the following types of non-personal data automatically:

  • Device Information: Information such as your device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to ensure compatibility and optimize game performance.
  • Game Performance Data: Anonymous crash reports and diagnostic data to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the game (e.g., session length, level progress) to balance difficulty and improve gameplay.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To Improve the Game: Analyzing crash logs and gameplay data to patch bugs and balance weapons/enemies.
  • To Provide Services: Necessary for saving game progress locally.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: If required by law, we may disclose your data to legal authorities.

3. Third-Party Services

We may employ third-party companies and services to facilitate our Game ("Service Providers"). These Service Providers are given access to your Personal Data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf.

Analytics

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
